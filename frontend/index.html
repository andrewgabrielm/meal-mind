About

MealMind plans Indian home meals under a hard budget using two integer programs (pack sizes & 48-hour surplus use), a Bayesian Weibull spoilage model, Shannon-entropy variety enforcement and ARIMA/GARCH price advisories. MDS482-4 · Christ University.
